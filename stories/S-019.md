**行业适用**：INT direct · BNK transferable（KYC 与多渠道支付的设计经验）· TEL transferable

## Situation
- Golden Bridge Drive, Nov 2020 – Jul 2022. A customisable, multi-tenant public platform for online-game businesses, built from 0 to 1: event operations, bonus incentives, VIP tiers, KYC and multi-channel payments.
- Constraints: time pressure, multi-tenancy, KYC across many jurisdictions.

## Task
- Project Manager & Architect: O2 (architecture) + O3 (delivery).

## Action
- I architected Spring Cloud microservices on Kubernetes behind an API gateway, on AWS, with modules for authentication, KYC, Bonus, analytics, VIP and Payment.
- I designed the rules at each level of the authentication service, and the bonus rules (eligibility, triggers, distribution).
- I designed hierarchical KYC identity verification after benchmarking competitor apps.

## Result
- The platform launched, with multi-jurisdiction KYC capability.
- **Do not use "200+ countries"** (IB-09: conflict inside 03).

## 讲法差异
- **ATL**：多租户隔离、规则模型怎么配置化、分级 KYC。
- **SM**：0 到 1 的架构取舍和时间压力。
