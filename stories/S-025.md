**行业适用**：INT direct · TEL transferable（B2G 投标与城市级部署）

## Situation
- AI Park, Apr 2016 – Sep 2017: a smart-parking IoT platform connecting car parks to the cloud for automated management, online payment and recommendations. Customers included city governments through bid processes.

## Task
- VP of Technical Development: led architecture, delivery, budget and the technical team, and the technical side of the bids.

## Action
- I led the technical bids for city parking projects.
- I introduced a SaaS model for future B2B/B2G customers.
- I launched the city pilots.

## Result
- Won city parking projects in Shijiazhuang, Handan and Beijing.
- Pilots launched in Shijiazhuang (2016), Handan (2017) and Beijing Chaoyang (2017).

## 讲法差异
- **SM**：B2G 商业模式和 SaaS 化决策。
- **TPM**：投标方案的准备，以及从试点到推广的节奏。
