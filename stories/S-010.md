**行业适用**：BNK direct · TEL transferable（受监管行业）

## Situation
- Delivery speed had to coexist with regulatory controls, and squads had to stay consistent across onshore and offshore.

## Task
- O3 + O4 for my own squads; O5 (contributed) to the bank-wide standards.

## Action
- I built the controls into the pipeline instead of adding manual checkpoints: quality gates on Bitbucket, Jenkins, SonarQube and Aldon, with JUnit, a SonarQube coverage gate, and automated regression and penetration testing in CI/CD.
- I ran secure SDLC, DPIA and data governance in line with MAS TRM.
- I contributed to bank-wide SDLC, coding and code-review standards, and to architecture-review governance beyond my own squads.
- I coached teams on new practices and tooling, including AI-assisted engineering.

## Result
- Audit-ready releases.
- **The coverage number is on hold**: "99% SonarQube coverage gate" is not used until IB-11 is resolved.

## 讲法差异
- **TPM**：把控制自动化，所以合规不拖慢节奏。
- **SM**：标准的影响力边界：只说 "contributed to"，不说 "set"。
- **ATL**：门禁规则怎么设，误报怎么处理。
