**行业适用**：BNK direct · INT / TEL transferable（大规模在线系统不停机迁移；须说明是迁移经验）

## Situation
- Live cross-border payment initiation (TT, RTGS) at a Tier-1 Singapore bank had to move from SWIFT MT to ISO 20022 MX under CBPR+, through the coexistence and MX-rich stages.
- Payments could not be disrupted inside cut-off windows. SWIFT's network end of MT/MX coexistence was Nov 2025 (public fact).
- Many dependencies: digital and branch channels, reference data, vendor-delivered and third-party banking systems.

## Task
- O2 (designed the approach) + O3 (owned programme delivery). Led a 15-engineer onshore/offshore team.

## Action
- I chose a phased, Blue/Green-style coexistence deployment instead of a big-bang cutover.
- I set up parallel traffic management and cutover-readiness gates before each release.
- I owned scope, roadmap, RAID, release planning and production rollout, and reported to senior management and the steering committee.
- After go-live I kept a steady release train for upgrades.

## Result
- MX-rich messaging went live in May and August 2025, ahead of SWIFT's November 2025 deadline.
- Coexistence mode was retired in August 2026, with no service interruption at the ISO 20022 cutover.
- Upgrades shipped every 2–3 months after go-live.

## 讲法差异
- **ATL**：为什么不 big-bang；共存期新旧两套报文并行时的流量管理；切换门槛怎么设。
- **TPM**：里程碑倒排（对齐 SWIFT 截止日）、RAID、发布计划、steering 汇报节奏。
- **SM**：关键平台现代化的风险决策：用分阶段换取可回退，以及共存退役的时机判断。
