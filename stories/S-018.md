**行业适用**：BNK direct · INT transferable（运营自动化）

## Situation
- Operations reconciled eMemo and quotation documents by hand, on a recurring basis.

## Task
- O1: personally wrote the agent, in April 2026.

## Action
- I built an AI agent that auto-generates validation prompts to check schema alignment and data-relationship consistency between eMemo and quotation documents.

## Result
- The recurring manual reconciliation task was removed. Time saved is not on record.
