## Situation
- Co-founded a vertical platform for licensed workplace and early-childhood learning audio, Oct 2017 – Sep 2019 (largely dormant from Apr 2019).

## Task
- Co-founder: owned product, architecture and technical delivery (O1 + O2), plus business development for content licensing.

## Action
- I took the product from market research to production.
- I introduced Spring Cloud microservices for extensibility.
- I brought licensed content onto the platform.

## Result
- Peak of ~100k daily active users; ~1,500 licensed audio titles and albums at launch.
- The company closed after fundraising failed.
