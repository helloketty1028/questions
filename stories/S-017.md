**行业适用**：BNK direct · INT transferable（AI 治理）

## Situation
- Putting an LLM agent into the engineering workflow of a MAS-regulated bank raises control, accountability and consistency questions.

## Task
- O1 + O2: owner of the service's design, including its governance model.

## Action
- I set the governance rule: the AI advises but never approves or merges. Human reviewers keep final authority, aligned with MAS FEAT principles.
- I combined deterministic static analysis (SonarQube) with the LLM review, so results do not depend on the model alone.
- I evaluated prompts through A/B testing.
- I took the service through formal architecture and risk approval before production, and briefed senior management with a 13-slide deck.

## Result
- Live in production since May 2026, after formal architecture and risk approval, under the human-in-the-loop rule.

## 讲法差异
- **SM**：为什么人保留合并权；FEAT 对齐；向管理层汇报什么。
- **TPM**：审批路径和上线门禁。
