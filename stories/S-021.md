**行业适用**：INT direct · BNK transferable（新建团队与平台的能力）

## Situation
- VV Technology, Apr 2019 – Jun 2020: a local-life platform (VV-Life). No team and no platform existed yet; both had to be built at the same time.

## Task
- Head of Technical Development: O2 plus people leadership.

## Action
- I built the technology organisation and the microservices platform (Spring Cloud, Kubernetes, Redis cluster, Elasticsearch, AWS) from scratch.
- I ran technology selection and owned the technology budget, and took part in product-strategy planning.
- I introduced CI/CD, code standards, and containerised deployment with rollback on Kubernetes.

## Result
- The first VV-Life release was delivered in 2019 (detail in S-022).
- Team size is not used (OQ-2.3).

## 讲法差异
