**行业适用**：INT direct · BNK / TEL transferable

## Situation
- A new 0-to-1 platform with a newly assembled team and fixed scope and budget expectations.

## Task
- O3: owned delivery budget, capacity and launch readiness.

## Action
- I built and led the project team.
- I introduced Agile/Scrum from scratch.
- I chose hybrid delivery: Waterfall for scope and budget, Agile sprints for execution.
- I set up CI on Jenkins and Amazon ECR.

## Result
- The platform launched. Team size is not used (IB-10).

## 讲法差异
- **TPM**：为什么用混合模式，节奏怎么设计，怎样做上线准备度评估。
