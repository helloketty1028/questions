**行业适用**：BNK direct · TEL transferable（运营商同样有 P1 和监管报告流程）

## Situation
- High-severity incidents in the payment platforms can involve several systems and vendors. My teams own production support.

## Task
- O3 + O4: I coordinate P1–P3 incidents within the bank's incident framework.

## Action
- I run escalation and stakeholder communication.
- I drive the investigation, the workaround and the permanent fix, and track SLAs.
- I lead the RCA and the post-incident documentation.
- I produce the technical incident analysis reports that feed the bank's regulatory reporting to MAS.

## Result
- Qualitative only. Specific incident details are confidential and stay out.

## 讲法差异
- **TPM**：沟通节奏、升级矩阵、谁对谁负责。
- **SM**：复盘机制如何转成长期改进（和 S-007 的 SLO、S-009 的 KPI 连起来讲）。
