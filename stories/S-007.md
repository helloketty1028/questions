**行业适用**：BNK direct · INT / TEL transferable

## Situation
- The payment platforms have no separate SRE or production-support team: my own teams own production support.

## Task
- O2: designed the monitoring and service-level practice my teams run.

## Action
- I built Splunk-based real-time transaction monitoring and alerting.
- I introduced SLIs/SLOs and SLA tracking, with capacity baselines.
- I introduced structured incident response and RCA.

## Result
- Qualitative: MTTR came down. **There is no before/after figure on record**, so say so directly.

## 讲法差异
- **ATL**：选什么 SLI，告警阈值怎么定，怎样避免告警噪音。
- **SM**：没有专职 SRE 时的组织设计："you build it, you run it"。
