**行业适用**：BNK direct · TEL transferable（监管 / 网络截止日驱动的项目群）

## Situation
- The ISO 20022 migration had a fixed external deadline (SWIFT's Nov 2025 end of coexistence) and ran under MAS TRM.
- It depended on channels, reference data, vendor-delivered and third-party systems, with onshore/offshore teams across time zones.

## Task
- O3: owned end-to-end programme delivery for a 15-engineer onshore/offshore team.

## Action
- I ran the programme on RAID: risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies tracked and reviewed with every party.
- I governed vendors and their SLAs on the critical path.
- I combined Agile delivery with change control, and reported status to senior management and the steering committee.
- I ran architecture trade-off forums (legacy vs modern) so design decisions were made explicitly and on time.

## Result
- Both MX-rich releases (May and August 2025) landed ahead of the November 2025 deadline.
- After go-live the programme kept shipping upgrades every 2–3 months.

## 讲法差异
- **TPM**：重点讲依赖地图、RAID 节奏、升级路径、steering 汇报内容。
- **SM**：讲治理机制本身：在固定截止日下，取舍是怎么做出来、由谁拍板的。
