**行业适用**：BNK direct · TEL transferable（运营商同样重度依赖外包供应商）

## Situation
- Several IT service vendors deliver into my platforms.

## Task
- Chair of vendor governance for four IT service vendors. Decision or joint-decision authority on vendor selection and SOW/licence negotiation, and owner of the delivery budget.

## Action
- I run periodic budget reviews with each vendor.
- I run year-end performance reviews.
- I hold accountability meetings with vendor staff and teams.
- I take part in vendor selection and SOW/licence negotiation as decision or joint-decision maker.

## Result
- Qualitative: a regular governance rhythm across four vendors. No metric on record.

## 讲法差异
- **TPM**：SLA 跟踪和问责会议怎么开。
- **SM**：商务权限与供应商组合策略。
