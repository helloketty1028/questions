**行业适用**：BNK direct · INT transferable（同样的控制也用在早期的 SaaS 和消费平台上）

## Situation
- Payment, lending and customer-data platforms hold sensitive data, and their data migrations ran under MAS TRM.

## Task
- O2: designed and led the security controls and the data migration.

## Action
- I led API-call encryption and decryption, database encryption of sensitive data, and dedicated credential-file management.
- I led the data migration for the payment platform and the customer-data / retail-lending platforms.
- I ran secure SDLC, DPIA and data governance in line with MAS TRM.
- I had applied the same set of controls at earlier employers (the SaaS and consumer platforms).

## Result
- Qualitative: the controls and migrations ran under MAS TRM governance. No metric on record.

## 讲法差异
- **ATL**：加密层次（传输、存储）、凭证管理、迁移校验。
- **TPM**：DPIA 和 TRM 的审批路径如何嵌进交付计划。
