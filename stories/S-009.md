**行业适用**：BNK direct · INT / TEL transferable（DORA 式指标是通用的）

## Situation
- Releases in a MAS-regulated bank must be measurable, governed and audit-ready.

## Task
- O3 + O4: set the delivery KPIs and release governance; governed through the squad leads.

## Action
- I set delivery KPIs: deployment frequency, MTTR, incident trend and change-failure rate.
- I put CI/CD release governance in place.
- I required SIT/UAT sign-off for every release.
- Before every production release on both platform lines, I personally prepare the change request (CRQ) and present it at the Change Advisory Board: go-live plan, risk controls, rollback plan, impact assessment, test results (SIT/UAT sign-off) and database changes.

## Result
- Audit-ready releases, with SIT/UAT sign-off on all releases.

## 讲法差异
- **SM**：为什么选这四个指标，以及指标怎样用于决策，而不是用来考核个人。
- **TPM**：发布门禁流程，以及签核链路上谁负责什么；CAB 讲解由我本人负责（PA-03.5）。
