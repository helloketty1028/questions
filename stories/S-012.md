**行业适用**：BNK direct · INT / TEL transferable

## Situation
- Two platform teams (a payment-initiation hub team and a customer-data team) across two platform lines, serving millions of accounts, with onshore and offshore engineers.

## Task
- People leader of 25 engineers (15 + 10), leading through squad leads and engineering managers, including dotted-line.

## Action
- I lead through the squad leads and engineering managers, and run 1:1s, mentoring and performance management.
- I work closely with teams in India, Thailand and Indonesia across time zones.
- I bring 5+ years of experience managing offshore teams.

## Result
- 25 engineers in two teams, two platform lines, millions of accounts.

## 讲法差异
- **SM**：leader of leaders：授权边界，以及怎样通过 squad lead 放大影响。
- **TPM**：跨时区协作机制：交接、例会时间、文档化。
