**行业适用**：INT direct（互联网平台里的支付子系统）· BNK transferable · TEL transferable（计费 / 对账类比）

## Situation
- VV-Life v1 needed a closed loop including payments, and the services had to stay fast under load.

## Task
- O2: designed the subsystem and the performance approach.

## Action
- I delivered the payment subsystem's transaction, management and reconciliation modules in the first release.
- I designed for performance: Spring Cloud microservices, Kafka-based asynchronous processing, and caching that kept requests off the database, with cache-breakdown protection.

## Result
- VV-Life v1 (closed loop, including payments) was delivered in 2019.
- Service response times reached the millisecond level. There is no % figure on record.
