**行业适用**：INT direct

## Situation
- Baidu Nuomi, 2014: the merchant-side group-buy management system (merchant contracts, group orders, coupons). I took it over as project leader.

## Task
- Project leader: O2 + project lead.

## Action
- I re-structured the architecture onto Dubbo microservices, with Resin as the front-end proxy.
- I led the web front-end, CMS development and QA teams.

## Result
- Delivered two months ahead of plan.

## 讲法差异
- **TPM**：接手后怎么重新排计划，怎样做到提前交付。
- **ATL**：为什么重构为 Dubbo 微服务。
