## ATL — 60s
> I'm a Java engineer by background, and I've stayed hands-on for 18 years. At UOB I led the end-to-end design of our payment-initiation hub — API contracts, orchestration, validation, enrichment and status tracking — and I personally wrote its core modules: transaction initiation, the corporate- and user-role permission matrix, and the processing of payment status results from downstream systems. I still review code every day across the platform's modules and co-design solutions with the engineers. This year I also designed and built an AI code-review service that's now in production; we estimate it cut senior engineers' review time by about 80%. Before UOB, I architected a multi-tenant SaaS platform from zero on Spring Cloud and Kubernetes. What I'm looking for is a role where architecture and hands-on depth are the main lever.

## TPM — 60s
> At UOB I'm a Technical Delivery Manager. My main responsibility is end-to-end delivery of mission-critical payment projects: delivery planning, technical design, CI/CD, SIT and UAT sign-off, risk management, production rollout and incident handling. The biggest was our ISO 20022 migration for cross-border payments. I chose a phased coexistence rather than a big-bang cutover, ran it on RAID with steering-committee reporting, and we took MX-rich messaging live in May and August 2025, ahead of SWIFT's November deadline. We retired coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption, and have shipped upgrades every two to three months since. I also chair governance for four IT service vendors. I'm now looking to bring that release governance and production-stability experience to another large-scale platform.

## SM — 60s
> I lead 25 engineers at UOB in two platform teams, across cross-border payments and retail lending, serving millions of accounts. I lead through squad leads and engineering managers, and I hold decision or joint-decision authority on technology investment, vendor selection and SOW negotiation. Before the bank, between 2016 and 2020, I held head-of-technology roles — building technology organisations and platforms from scratch, including a payments subsystem and a smart-parking IoT platform piloted in three cities. And I still bring innovation in: this year I built an AI code-review service in production, with an estimated 80% cut in senior review time. I'm looking for a broader remit — owning technology across more of the business.
