## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先澄清：量级、日内还是日终、cut-off、SLA、谁处理断点。
2. 接入：内部（交易表、总账、发送记录）和外部（camt.053 / 054、pacs.002；证券是托管行 / CSD 对账单）。
3. 标准化：统一模型，用稳定参考号（UETR、EndToEndId）做 key。
4. 匹配：先精确 key，再容差规则；断点进入工作流（有人负责、有账龄、有自动修复规则）。
5. 全程幂等、可审计；你在 VV 做过对账模块。

## 30s
> First I'd clarify volumes, latency, cut-offs and SLAs. Then: ingest the internal side — transaction records and the ledger — and the external side, like statements from correspondents or custodians; normalise formats; run a matching engine on keys with tolerances; and send breaks to a workflow with ownership, ageing and auto-resolution rules. Everything idempotent and auditable.

## ATL — 60s
> I'd start by clarifying: volumes, timeliness — intraday or end of day — cut-offs, SLAs, and who works the breaks. Then the design. Ingestion: the internal side — transaction records, ledger postings, sent-message logs — and the external side: for cross-border payments, camt.053 and camt.054 statements and pacs.002 statuses; for securities, custodian or CSD statements. Normalise everything into one model keyed on stable references like UETR or end-to-end ID. A matching engine runs exact keys first, then tolerance rules on amount and value date. Unmatched items become breaks in a workflow with owners, ageing and auto-resolution rules for known patterns. Everything is idempotent and auditable. I built reconciliation modules into a payment subsystem at VV, so I know break handling is where most of the real work sits.
