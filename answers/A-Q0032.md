## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 数据：哪些数据能发给 AI；客户数据、PII、凭证、生产数据一律不进。
2. 输出：AI 结果是建议，走同样的控制。
3. 可追溯：输入、输出、谁批准的。
4. AI 服务本身的安全控制。
5. 【SA-03 入库后启用】你的服务的实际数据边界。

## 30s
> Responsible AI in a bank starts with data. Only approved tools; no customer data, PII, credentials or production data in prompts; mask where needed. Then control the output: AI results go through the same testing, review and security controls. Keep traceability — what went in, what came out, who approved. And a human stays accountable. In my AI review service, that's aligned with MAS FEAT principles.

## ATL — 60s
> Four layers. First, data: decide what may be sent at all. Customer data, PII, credentials and production data never go into a prompt; if something sensitive is needed, mask it first. Second, the output: AI results are suggestions and go through the same testing, code review and security scanning as everything else. Third, traceability: we should be able to see what input was given, what the AI produced and who approved the final change. Fourth, the AI service itself needs normal controls — authentication, authorisation, monitoring, rate limiting and failure handling.
> 【SA-03 入库后启用：In our review service the agent runs isolated and read-only with no outbound network; code stays inside the bank's network, the model endpoint is in-region, and every prompt and output is logged.】

## SM — 60s
> I see responsible AI as governance, not a tool setting. In my AI code-review service, the rule is that the AI advises and never approves or merges — human reviewers keep final authority, in line with MAS FEAT principles — and it went through formal architecture and risk approval before production. Beyond that: approved tools only, clear rules on which data may be used, an audit trail of inputs and outputs, and a named human accountable for every decision the AI informs. That's what lets a regulated bank use AI at all.
