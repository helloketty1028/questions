## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先说清楚哪些是测量的、哪些是估算的。
2. 你的 80% 是估算，讲公式。
3. 以后要看的指标：PR cycle time、review turnaround、缺陷泄漏、DORA（你已经在用 DORA 式 KPI，PA-03.1）。
4. 对待抵触：不强推；AI 只建议；让持怀疑态度的资深工程师来审 AI 的输出（建议做法）。

## 30s
> I measure outcomes, not activity — never lines of code. For our AI review service, I use an estimate I can explain: human review rounds fell to one or two per PR, each about 30% as long, which works out to roughly an 80% cut in senior review time. On resistance, I don't force it: comments are advisory, and I'd make the sceptical seniors the reviewers of the AI's output.

## ATL — 60s
> Technically, I'd measure what the tool changes, not how much it's used: review rounds per PR, review turnaround, findings accepted versus rejected, and defects that still reach SIT or production. For our service, the honest figure is an estimate — about 80% less senior review time — and I can show the formula behind it. On resistance: engineers trust what they can verify. I improved the prompts through A/B testing, and keeping the AI advisory-only means a sceptical engineer can simply ignore a bad suggestion, which lowers the cost of trying it.

## SM — 60s
> Two parts. Measuring: I'm explicit about what's measured and what's estimated. For our review service, senior review time fell by an estimated 80% — before, a PR needed several human rounds; after, one or two, each about 30% as long, because the AI clears the basics. Going further, I'd track PR cycle time, review turnaround, defect leakage and DORA-style metrics like lead time and change-failure rate, which I already use for delivery. Resistance: I'd never force it. The comments are advisory, so nobody loses control, and I'd put the most sceptical senior engineers in charge of reviewing the AI's output — their experience becomes the guardrails.
