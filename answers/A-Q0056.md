## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 对人灵活，对结果严格；放手执行，守住风险和质量。
2. 看两件事：能不能做？愿不愿意担？
3. 四种组合四种管法：都高 → 授权；能做但不主动 → 讲清期望并跟进；想做但还不会 → 教练；都低 → 明确任务、经常检查。
4. 先查原因，再贴标签：是能力、态度，还是资源问题？

## TPM — 60s
> In delivery, my style is clear ownership with close visibility. Every piece of work has one owner, a goal, a deadline and the boundaries on risk and quality. How they get there is up to them, but risks and blockers come to me early. I adjust to the person: experienced leads get space, people still learning get coaching, and where commitment is weak I make expectations explicit and follow up. The results bar is the same for everyone.

## SM — 60s
> My style is simple. I'm flexible with people, but strict about results: I hand over the work, but I hold the line on risk and quality. And I don't manage everyone the same way. I look at two things: can they do it, and do they want to own it? If both are high, I give the goal and the boundaries, and I step back — "You own it; keep me posted on the risks and blockers." If they can do it but don't step up, I make the expectation very clear, and I follow up. If they want to but are still learning, I coach — they come back with their own plan first, then I guide. If both are low, I give clear tasks and deadlines, and I check often. But first, I find out why: is it skill, attitude, or missing resources?
