## ATL — 60s
> Use cases: hot reads that are expensive to compute or fetch — reference data, user or merchant profiles, sessions — plus things like rate limiting and distributed locks. At VV Technology and AI Park I put caching in front of the database to keep requests off it, together with Kafka for asynchronous flows, and that brought service processing to the millisecond level. At Sohu I led the migration of the CMS from a memcached cluster to a Redis cluster. The risks are the classic three: breakdown when a hot key expires, avalanche when many keys expire together, and penetration from requests for keys that don't exist. Mitigations: a mutex or single-flight rebuild, jittered TTLs, and caching nulls or using a Bloom filter. For invalidation I prefer cache-aside with explicit eviction on write.
