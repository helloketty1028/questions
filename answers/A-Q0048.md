## ATL — 60s
> The technical side of a bet is making it reversible. At VV, for a 0-to-1 platform, I chose Spring Cloud microservices so we could change one part without touching the rest, Kafka for asynchronous flows, and caching to keep the database safe as load grew. CI/CD and containerised deployment with rollback on Kubernetes meant a bad release cost minutes, not days. That let us deliver the first VV-Life release, payments included, in 2019, and keep iterating from there.
