## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 原则：合规、恰好一次的效果（幂等）、高峰下的低延迟。
2. 组件：接入 → schema 和业务校验 → enrichment → 路由到合适的通道 → 状态跟踪（含 pain.002）。
3. 技术选型：Spring Boot；关键支付路径用 MQ 保证投递；Kafka 做事件、审计和下游分发；关系库存状态；Redis 做热查找（BIC 等）。
4. 取舍：选成熟技术——在支付里，无聊是优点。
5. 最后一句连到你自己的 hub（A-Q0029）。

## 30s
> Three layers: principles, components, trade-offs. Principles: regulatory compliance, exactly-once effect through idempotency, and low latency at peak. Components: intake, schema and business validation, enrichment, routing to the right rail, and status tracking including pain.002. Technology: Spring Boot services; guaranteed-delivery messaging on the payment path; Kafka for events, audit and fan-out; a relational database for transaction state; Redis for hot lookups like BIC codes. And proven stacks over new ones — in payments, boring is a feature.

## ATL — 60s
> Principles first: compliance, an exactly-once effect — achieved through idempotency, because the network only gives you at-least-once — and low latency under peak load. Components: intake from channels; schema and business validation; enrichment from reference data like FX rates, accounts and customer data; routing to the right rail; and status tracking, processing pain.002 and other status messages back into a state machine. Technology mapping: Spring Boot services; guaranteed-delivery messaging, such as IBM MQ, on the critical payment path; Kafka for status events, the audit trail and downstream fan-out; a relational database for transaction state; Redis for hot lookups like BIC codes. Trade-off: proven stacks over new ones. I designed the hub at UOB along these lines — it covers initiation, validation, enrichment, authorisation and status tracking, with processing and settlement downstream — and I tuned its thread pools and JVM for latency under load.

## 追问与应对
- **Q**：Exactly-once? / **A**：网络只保证 at-least-once → 唯一业务参考号 + 幂等消费 + 状态机，再加对账（见 A-Q0036）。
- **Q**：Kafka or MQ? / **A**：关键支付路径用 MQ（逐条可靠投递、事务语义）；事件分发、审计、重放用 Kafka。
