## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 实操来源：GBD（多租户 SaaS 跑在 AWS 上）、VV（AWS 上的微服务平台）。
2. 韧性默认做法：multi-AZ；DR 按 RTO / RPO 决定是否 multi-region；最小权限 IAM；加密；CloudWatch；基础设施即代码。
3. 银行语境：MAS 对关键系统要求 RTO 不超过 4 小时。
4. 没有 AWS 认证，不回避。

## 30s
> My hands-on AWS work is from Golden Bridge and VV Technology. At Golden Bridge I architected a multi-tenant SaaS platform on AWS — API Gateway, EC2 and ECS, SQS and SNS, RDS, DynamoDB, ElastiCache, Lambda, CloudWatch and IAM — with CI on Jenkins and Amazon ECR. ## ATL — 60s
> Most of my AWS work is from Golden Bridge and VV. At Golden Bridge I architected a multi-tenant SaaS platform from zero: Spring Cloud microservices on Kubernetes behind an API gateway, using Route 53, API Gateway, EC2 and ECS, SQS and SNS, S3, RDS, DynamoDB, ElastiCache, Lambda, Cognito, CloudWatch and IAM, with CI on Jenkins and Amazon ECR. At VV I built the microservices platform on AWS, with containerised deployment and rollback on Kubernetes. My resilience approach: multi-AZ by default; multi-region DR where the RTO and RPO justify it; health checks and auto scaling; least-privilege IAM and encryption; CloudWatch alarms; and infrastructure as code so environments are reproducible. In a Singapore bank I'd also design to MAS's limit: an RTO of no more than four hours for critical systems.
