## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 机制：主持 4 家 IT 服务供应商的治理——定期预算评审、年终绩效评审、问责会议。
2. 权限：供应商选择和 SOW / license 谈判上有决策或共同决策权。
3. 日常：管 SLA（PA-01 §6）。
4. 不说供应商名字。

## 30s
> At UOB I chair governance for four IT service vendors: periodic budget reviews, year-end performance reviews and accountability meetings with their teams. I hold decision or joint-decision authority on vendor selection and SOW and licence negotiation, and I govern their SLAs on our critical paths.

## ATL — 60s
> From the technical side, I hold vendors to the same engineering bar as everyone else. I review the code delivered on our platform every day and co-design the solutions with the engineers, so quality problems surface in review, not in production. The pipeline gates — unit tests, SonarQube, regression and security testing — apply to every team, whoever employs them. That gives me facts, not impressions, for the governance meetings I chair for four IT service vendors: budget reviews, year-end performance reviews and accountability sessions.

## TPM — 60s
> I run vendors on a regular governance rhythm. I chair governance for four IT service vendors: periodic budget reviews, year-end performance reviews, and accountability meetings with their staff and teams. On delivery, I track their SLAs on our critical path. In the ISO 20022 programme, vendor-delivered and third-party systems were key dependencies, so they sat in our RAID and steering-committee reporting exactly like internal teams — same milestones, same SIT and UAT gates, same change approval. There's no "vendor side" and "our side" on a release: one plan, one set of gates.

## SM — 60s
> I treat vendors as part of the delivery system, governed on commercial and performance terms. I hold decision or joint-decision authority on vendor selection and on SOW and licence negotiation, and I own the delivery budget. I chair governance for four IT service vendors — budget, year-end performance and accountability. The aim is a vendor portfolio we can hold to outcomes, not just headcount: clear SLAs, regular performance reviews, and the leverage of the commercial relationship when something isn't working.

## 追问与应对
- **Q**：Tell me about a vendor that underperformed. / **A**：03 没有具体案例（IB-02）。**不要临场编**，可以说"I'd rather describe the mechanism"，或者等案例入库。
- **Q**：Are your engineers vendor staff? / **A**：见待确认 SC-01。
- **Q**：How do you choose outsourcing vendors? / **A**：（IFS #7 JD-2 的思路）"It depends on the model: if they sit with my team, I interview the people as I would a hire; if it's project-based, I look at the company — domain track record, real code, and who they'll actually put on the project. Anything touching customer data stays in our environment. The contract covers source-code ownership, full documentation, an exit clause and audit rights — if I can't hand the work to someone else, I don't own it." 评估方法见 A-Q0041。
