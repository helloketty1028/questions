## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 三个方面：范围控制、早识别风险、严格执行跟踪。
2. 范围：和业务、BA、架构师一起把 MVP 定清楚（X-02）。
3. 风险：环境就绪、上下游依赖、SIT / UAT 延误、资源缺口（RAID，PA-01.5）。
4. 执行：每个行动项都有明确负责人，持续跟踪。
5. 结果：ISO 20022 提前于截止日上线。

## 30s
> Three things: clear scope, early risk management and strict execution. First, I agree the MVP scope with the business, BAs and architects. Second, I push the team to surface risks as early as possible — environment readiness, upstream and downstream dependencies, SIT and UAT delays, resource gaps. Third, every action item has a clear owner and is tracked closely. That's how our ISO 20022 migration went live ahead of SWIFT's deadline.

## TPM — 60s
> On our ISO 20022 migration the SWIFT deadline was fixed, so I focused on three areas. Scope: I worked with the business, BAs and architects to define the MVP for each release clearly — what must ship by the date. Risk: I pushed teams to raise risks as early as possible — environment readiness, upstream and downstream dependencies, SIT and UAT delays, resource gaps — and ran them on RAID with steering-committee reporting. Execution: every action item had a clear owner, and we tracked defects and progress closely. We took MX-rich messaging live in May and August 2025, ahead of the November deadline, and retired coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption.

## SM — 60s
> I set up the conditions for meeting the date rather than chasing it at the end: a clearly agreed MVP, risks surfaced early and owned, and a steering rhythm where trade-offs get decided quickly. On our ISO 20022 programme, that meant a phased coexistence instead of one big cutover, so each release was smaller and safer. We beat SWIFT's deadline and retired coexistence with no service interruption.
