## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先肯定在 UOB 的收获（不评价雇主）。
2. 给一个客观时点：ISO 20022 项目已经收尾（2026 年 8 月退出共存）。
3. **按岗位给动机**（三个版本都必须是你的真实想法；同一家公司各轮面试只用一个版本）。
4. 收尾：新岗位怎么满足这个动机。

## 30s
> I've gained valuable experience at UOB over the past four years, especially delivering complex payment projects on business-critical platforms with many stakeholders. Our ISO 20022 programme has now reached its end — we retired the coexistence mode in August 2026 — so it's a natural point to broaden my experience and take on new challenges.

## ATL — 60s
> I've been with UOB for four years. It's my first banking role, and it's been a very good experience — I've learned a lot, and my manager has been supportive. Technically it's been some of the deepest work of my career: I designed our payment-initiation hub and wrote its core modules, and I built an AI code-review service that's now in production. For my next step, I'm looking to work on larger-scale systems and more complex technical challenges. I want to stay hands-on while continuing to grow as a technical leader. So it's less about leaving UOB, and more about where I want to grow next.

## TPM — 60s
> I've spent four years at UOB delivering payment projects. The biggest was our ISO 20022 migration for cross-border payments: we took MX-rich messaging live in May and August 2025, ahead of SWIFT's deadline, and retired coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption. Along the way I've strengthened both my delivery skills and my understanding of payment systems, working with business-critical platforms and many stakeholders. With that programme complete, I'd like to broaden my business domain beyond payments. The delivery discipline I've built — release governance, change approval, incident handling — transfers well, and I want to apply it to a new kind of business-critical platform.

## SM — 60s
> At UOB I lead two platform lines — cross-border payments and retail lending — with 25 engineers, serving millions of accounts. It's been valuable: I've learned how a Tier-1 bank governs technology, from MAS TRM to vendor governance. But in a bank that size, I'm one part of a very large machine. I'd like broader ownership: a larger team, a wider business scope, and a direct line from technology decisions to business outcomes. Before UOB I held head-of-technology roles and built teams and platforms from scratch, so I know what that broader remit involves. Our ISO 20022 programme has just reached its end, so this is the right time to make that move.

## 行业角度（每个行业 1–2 句，说明同一个故事怎么换侧重点）
- **BNK**：留在受监管金融行业，但从支付扩展到更多业务线（贷款、财富、市场）。
- **BNK · fintech**：想把银行级的严谨带到更快的环境里："Tier-1 rigour at start-up speed."
- **INT**："My career started in internet companies; I'd like to combine that product speed with what I learned in banking."
- **TEL**：新行业，但计费和支付一样都是钱的流动，同样是业务关键平台。
- **BNK · global bank（如 JPM）**："larger scale and more complex technical challenges" 加一句为什么是这家（见 A-Q0005）。
- **BNK · fintech（大行 → 中小金融机构，如 IFS）**：用 SM 版，收尾加一句 "What I want next is end-to-end ownership — the whole technology function, not one piece of it."
- **ATL / IC 岗（Visa、Cloudera 实录）**："I'm looking for a more technical, architecture-focused role"——和 ATL 版一致。
- **global bank（#15 通用模板）**：可以说想获得国际银行的环境，但**不说 "UOB is still a local bank"**——UOB 是有区域业务的银行，这样说既不准确也像在贬低前雇主。
