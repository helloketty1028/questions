## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 范围：ISO 20022 项目中从老平台迁移支付数据和相关数据（X-01）。
2. 控制：敏感数据加密、凭证管理、MAS TRM 下的 secure SDLC 和 DPIA。
3. 【SD-05 入库后】entitlement 数据：用户、公司、组、角色；审批 → 导出 → 逐条校验 → 批处理导入。
4. 验证：逐条校验 + 共存期双方对账（SI-03）。

## 30s
> On our ISO 20022 programme, I led the migration of payment data and related data from the legacy platform, with the security controls around it: encryption of sensitive data, credential management, and secure SDLC and DPIA under MAS TRM. The rule was that nothing moved without validation — and old and new ran side by side in coexistence, so we could check before switching.

## ATL — 60s
> The migration moved data from the legacy platform onto our new payment-initiation hub. Technically, the controls came first: sensitive fields encrypted in the database, API-level encryption, and dedicated credential management.
> 【SD-05 入库后启用：The hardest part was entitlement data — users, companies, groups and roles, where one user can belong to several groups across companies. Each batch went through an approval step in the old system, was exported as JSON, then validated record by record on the new side against the related applications — user accounts, remittance — before Spring Batch jobs loaded it.】
> Because both systems ran in coexistence, we could verify behaviour before cutting over.

## TPM — 60s
> I ran it as a controlled change, not a data copy. Scope was agreed per batch; every batch needed approval before extraction; validation results were reviewed before load; and the whole thing ran under secure SDLC and DPIA in line with MAS TRM. Coexistence meant old and new ran side by side, so the business never depended on an unverified migration. 【SI-03 入库后启用：During coexistence both sides were reconciled, so no record could drift between them.】
