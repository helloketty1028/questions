## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 承认上次的面试：确实面过，收获是什么。
2. 之后有什么变化：按你的真实情况填。
3. 为什么这个岗位更匹配。

## 30s

Since then I've [what you've genuinely deepened], and our AI code-review service has kept running in production, so I can speak to its results. This role is a better match, because it's centred on exactly that kind of hands-on and AI-assisted engineering.
