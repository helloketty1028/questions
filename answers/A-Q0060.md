> Honestly, yes — one. At UOB I have a lot behind me: a full CI/CD pipeline with quality gates, compliance tooling, a change-approval process where I present every release at CAB, and review functions around all of it. I don't know yet what exists here. My worry isn't having less; it's assuming something is there when it isn't. So in my first weeks I'd check what's actually in place — pipeline, testing, access controls, incident process — and build only what's missing, starting with whatever carries the most risk.
