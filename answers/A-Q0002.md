## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先问预算区间，不先报点值。
2. 给期望区间（每次面试前自己定，**数字不入库**）。
3. 谈总包：base、bonus、benefits。
4. 被追问当前薪资：如实给结构，入职时对方可能核对工资单。
5. 预算低于现薪时：只在真实愿意的情况下表态灵活。

## 30s
> Before I give a number, could I ask what range is budgeted for this role? My expectation is around [range] a month, but I look at the total package — base, bonus and benefits — and I'm open to a sensible discussion if the role is the right fit.

## ATL — 60s
> （行政题，三个岗位相同，见 30s。）
> If asked about current pay: "My current base is [X] a month, plus an annual performance bonus."
> If the budget is below my current package (use only if true): "That's below my current package, but for the right role I'm flexible. What matters more to me is the scope and the long-term fit."

## TPM — 60s
> （同上。）

## SM — 60s
> （同上；高管岗可以加一句）"Beyond base, I'd like to understand the bonus structure and any long-term incentive."

## 行业角度（每个行业 1–2 句，说明同一个故事怎么换侧重点）
- **BNK**：问清 base、AWS（13 薪）和绩效 bonus 的月数。
- **BNK · fintech**：问清现金与股权 / 期权的比例，以及 vesting。
- **INT**：股权 / RSU 通常占比大，要按总包比较。
- **TEL**：问清浮动 bonus 的区间和发放依据。

## 追问与应对
- **Q**：What's the lowest you would accept? / **A**："I'd rather look at the whole package together — could you share the full structure first?"
