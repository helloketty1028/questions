## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 三个理由，固定顺序：**岗位 → 业务领域 → 公司**。
2. 岗位：和你每天做的事有什么重合，所以第一天就能贡献。
3. 业务：这个领域为什么重要，和你看护过的系统有什么相似。
4. 公司：一个具体的、查证过的公司事实，加上你的观点（观点要说明是观点）。
5. 新行业要直说："is new to me, and I'm ready to learn it."

Second, the business — [domain] is where [money / customers / risk] flows, just like payments in a bank. Third, the company — [one verified fact about the company's direction], and I'd like to be part of that.

## ATL — 60s
> Three reasons. First, the technical problem: [platform] has to stay [accurate / available / scalable] under real load, with many downstream integrations — that's the kind of system I design and still code for. On our payment hub I wrote the initiation, permission and status-processing cores. Second, the domain: [domain] is new to me in its details, but the engineering problems are familiar — transaction accuracy, status tracking, safe change on a live platform. Third, the company: [verified fact about its direction], which means real architecture change ahead. That's where I add the most value: designing the target state and getting it into production safely.

## TPM — 60s
> （Singtel BCC 实例，改公司名和业务即可复用）
> Three reasons. First, the role. It's very close to what I do every day at UOB: I manage releases for a business-critical platform — scope, SIT and UAT sign-off, change requests and CAB, cutover and production support. Second, BCC. Billing and charging is where the money flows in a telco, just like payments in a bank. If something goes wrong, it hits revenue and customer trust straight away — that's the kind of system I've looked after for four years. Third, Singtel itself. With Singtel28 the company is simplifying its products and using AI to improve customer experience, and I'd expect much of that change to land in billing. The telco side is new to me, and I'm ready to learn it.

Second, the scale of change ahead: [company] is in the middle of [verified strategic change], and technology is on the critical path. Third, ownership: the role gives me [remit] — the chance to set direction, build the team and be accountable for outcomes, not just one platform line. At UOB I've learned how a Tier-1 bank governs technology; before that I built teams and platforms from scratch. This role uses both.

## 行业角度（每个行业 1–2 句，说明同一个故事怎么换侧重点）
- **BNK**：监管驱动的现代化（ISO 20022 这类）、可用性和合规；公司事实挑数字化或平台改造计划。
- **BNK · fintech**：增长速度加牌照 / 合规；强调你同时懂两边。
- **INT**：规模和产品速度；公司事实挑产品或用户增长方向。
- **TEL**：计费 = 收入；公司事实用 Singtel28（见 domain-notes/TEL_billing-charging.md）。"落到计费"是你的推断，要当作观点来说。
- **BNK · global bank 实例（JPM Post Trade，旧稿 #4）**："one of the few firms that's core to global payments and top-tier in securities and custody at the same time"，加上岗位匹配："hands-on in design and delivery while leading the team"。如果 JD 明确以 AI-assisted engineering 为重点，可以加上这一条。
