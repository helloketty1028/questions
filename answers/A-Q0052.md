## SM — 60s
> 【SI-01 入库后启用——你 9/29 定稿的 70 秒版】
> Let me give you a concrete example. At VV Technology, I had a lead who ran several teams, including our big-data team, and he was not performing as expected. His teams were blocking the whole project: our business analysts needed their support to move forward, and for months there was no progress. The problem was the lead, not the teams. He had gaps in planning and in reporting upward, and he brought personal emotion into the team. His people became disengaged, and some of them left. So I sat down with him and gave him the facts, not opinions. We agreed on clear goals and a review period, and I gave him real support and a real chance. In the end he had not met them, so I let him go. It was not a nice decision, but it was a fair one. The team started moving again within weeks.
