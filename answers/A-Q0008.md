## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先承认看起来像落差。
2. 理由：看重的是岗位和价值，不是头衔和团队规模。
3. 证据：你现在就是不靠汇报线去协调团队和供应商。
4. 收尾：长期意愿，不只是下一个头衔（和 A-Q0002 的薪资灵活度口径一致）。

## 30s
> I understand it may look narrower than my current role. But for me it's about the role and the value I can bring, not the title or team size. At UOB I already work across squad leads, other teams and four vendors to get changes delivered safely — much of that is influence without direct authority. I'm looking for a place to contribute over the long term, not just my next job title.

## ATL — 60s
> （目标岗是个人贡献者 / 架构师时）Honestly, the part of my job I enjoy most is the technical part. Even as a delivery manager I review code every day, co-design solutions with the engineers, own the architecture, and write the core modules when significant changes land — on our payment hub that included the initiation, permission and status-processing cores. This year I also designed and built an AI code-review service that's now in production. A role where that's the main job, not one part of it, is a step towards what I do best, not a step down. And I'm looking for a place to do it for the long term.

## TPM — 60s
> I understand this may look narrower than my current position. But for me it's more about the role and the value I can bring than the title or team size. At UOB I already work across different teams, squad leads and vendors to make sure changes are delivered safely, and I chair governance for four IT service vendors — most of that is coordination without direct reporting lines. I believe that's exactly what this role needs. I'm also interested in learning a new business and applying my experience to another business-critical platform. I'm looking for the right role where I can contribute and grow over the long term, not just my next job title.

## SM — 60s
> （目标岗头衔较低、但范围更宽时，例如小型金融公司）The size of the remit matters more to me than the title. At UOB I lead two platform lines inside a very large organisation, and many decisions sit above or around me. Here I'd own technology across more of the business, with a direct line to outcomes. I've done that before: between 2016 and 2020 I held head-of-technology roles and built teams and platforms from scratch. And the bank has taught me governance — MAS TRM, vendor management, safe change. Bringing both into a broader role is a bigger job, even if the title is smaller.
