## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 边界：hub 负责 initiation、validation、enrichment、authorisation、status tracking；processing 和 settlement 在下游。
2. 输入：网银和分行渠道，加上参考数据（FX rate、account、customer）。
3. 内部：Java、Spring Boot、Apache Camel；你亲手写的三个核心模块。
4. 下游：SWIFT ISO 20022 flow、CASA；pain.002 回传状态。
5. 韧性和取舍：【SA-01 入库后启用】幂等、本地参考数据、异步移交。

## 30s
> I'll take the cross-border payment-initiation hub I designed at UOB. It takes payments from Internet Banking and branch channels, validates them, enriches them with reference data — FX rates, account and customer data — applies authorisation, and hands them to downstream systems, including the SWIFT ISO 20022 flow. It tracks status through pain.002 messages. Processing and settlement sit outside it. It runs at 99.9%-plus availability.

## ATL — 60s
> Let me start with the boundary. The hub owns initiation, validation, enrichment, authorisation and status tracking; processing and settlement sit downstream. Inputs are Internet Banking and branch channels, plus reference data — FX rates, account and customer. Inside, it's Java and Spring Boot, with Apache Camel for integration and orchestration. Authorisation is a permission matrix of corporate roles and user roles, which I wrote myself, along with transaction initiation and the processing of status results coming back from downstream. It hands payments to the SWIFT ISO 20022 flow and CASA, and status comes back as pain.002.
> 【SA-01 入库后启用：Three design points. Idempotency — every payment carries a unique business reference enforced in the database, plus an explicit state machine, so a retry never creates a duplicate. Dependency isolation — reference data is replicated locally, keeping source systems off the critical path; the trade-off is freshness. Resiliency — the hand-off is asynchronous, with retry and an exception queue, and on a timeout we check downstream status before resending.】

## TPM — 60s
> The hub sits between our channels and the downstream payment systems. It takes payments from Internet Banking and branches, validates and enriches them with reference data, applies authorisation, hands them to the SWIFT ISO 20022 flow and other downstream systems, and tracks their status. From a delivery view, this architecture is mostly about dependencies: channels, reference data, vendor-delivered and third-party systems all touch it. That's why our ISO 20022 migration ran as a phased coexistence — MT and MX side by side, with readiness gates — rather than a big-bang cutover. It went live in May and August 2025 and retired coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption.

## SM — 60s
> It's a payment-initiation hub: one place where all cross-border payments from our channels are validated, enriched, authorised and tracked before they go downstream to SWIFT and other systems. The architectural decision I'd highlight is the boundary itself. Centralising authorisation, validation and status in one hub gives consistency, a single audit trail and reuse across channels, while processing and settlement stay with the systems built for them. That boundary is also what let us modernise it to ISO 20022 in phases without disrupting payments. It runs at 99.9%-plus availability.

## 追问与应对
- **Q**：How do you prevent duplicate payments? / **A**：【SA-01 入库后】唯一业务参考号由数据库约束保证，加状态机；入库前讲原则："unique reference, idempotent processing, and reconciliation as the final control"。
- **Q**：What if the SWIFT gateway or a downstream system is down? / **A**：03 没有记录具体机制；【SA-01 入库后】异步移交 + 重试 + exception queue，超时先查下游状态。
- **Q**：Where's the bottleneck? / **A**：PA-01.4：高负载下的时延；做法是调 thread pool 和 JVM、去掉同步阻塞（没有数字）。
- **Q**：How do services communicate? / **A**：Apache Camel 做集成和编排，event-driven integration（PA-01 §3）。
