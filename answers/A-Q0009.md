## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先承认缺口："Not in telco."
2. 三座桥：交易准确性和 cut-off；催收（PA-04.1）；对账（VV，PA-11.2）。
3. 发布纪律和行业无关。
4. 给 30–60 天的学习计划。

## 30s
> Not in telco. But the core problems are familiar. In payments every transaction must be accurate, traceable and inside cut-off windows, and billing runs need the same discipline. I own the technology for post-disbursement collections across credit-card and unsecured-loan portfolios, which is close to receivables and collections in a BSS stack. And I'd use my first 30 to 60 days to learn the billing cycles, rating and charging, and the downstream impacts.

## ATL — 60s
> Not in telco, but the engineering problems overlap. At UOB I wrote the status-processing module for payment results coming back from downstream systems — making sure every transaction ends in a correct, traceable state. Earlier, at VV Technology, I delivered a payment subsystem with transaction, management and reconciliation modules, and on our customer-360 platform I built Spring Batch scheduled jobs — the same pattern as billing runs. What I'd need to learn is the telco-specific part: rating rules, real-time charging for prepaid, and the bill cycle. I'd start with the end-to-end flow and the platform's incident history.

## TPM — 60s
> I don't have direct experience in telecom billing or BSS, but I have relevant experience in similar areas. In banking, accuracy, traceability and strict cut-off times are critical. I own technology for collections across credit-card and unsecured-loan portfolios. I've led a customer-360 platform team, and earlier I delivered a payment subsystem with transaction, management and reconciliation modules. The business processes are different, but many delivery challenges are similar, and release discipline itself is domain-agnostic — the same SIT, UAT, change-approval and cutover gates apply. I'd use my first 30 to 60 days to understand BCC's billing cycles, rating and charging, and downstream dependencies.

## SM — 60s
> Not in telco. What I bring is experience running platforms where an error hits customers and money directly — cross-border payments and credit-card and loan collections at a Tier-1 bank, serving millions of accounts. Billing is revenue, and it needs the same governance: accuracy, auditability and safe change. That's what my last four years have been built on, and earlier I delivered a payment subsystem with reconciliation at a start-up. I'd close the domain gap fast: in my first two months I'd lean on the squad leads and the business, learn the billing cycle and charging flows, and agree with them where the biggest release risks are.

## 行业角度（每个行业 1–2 句，说明同一个故事怎么换侧重点）
