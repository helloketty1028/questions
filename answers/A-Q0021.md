## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. "Both, pragmatically."
2. 三个例子：customer-360 平台用 Waterfall 治理；ISO 20022 项目在 squad 里用 Agile，外加变更控制和固定发布门禁；GBD 在混合模式里从零建立 Scrum。
3. 映射到目标岗位：squad 跑 Agile，发布列车保留固定的治理节点。

## 30s
> Both, pragmatically. Our customer-360 platform runs under waterfall governance. The ISO 20022 programme used agile delivery inside the squads, with change control and fixed release gates. At Golden Bridge I set up Scrum from scratch inside a hybrid model: waterfall for scope and budget, sprints for the build.

## ATL — 60s
> I choose by the shape of the risk. Where requirements are fixed and regulated — data models on our customer-360 platform — waterfall-style design reviews pay off. Where the design evolves, as in the ISO 20022 hub, squads iterate in sprints, but architecture decisions still go through explicit trade-off forums. At Golden Bridge, building a platform from zero, I set up Scrum from scratch — there, fast iteration was right because the design was still being discovered. The method serves the architecture, not the other way round.

## TPM — 60s
> Both, pragmatically. Our customer-360 platform runs under waterfall governance. The ISO 20022 programme used agile delivery inside the squads, with change control and fixed release gates. At Golden Bridge I set up Scrum from scratch inside a hybrid model: waterfall for scope and budget, sprints for the build. Whatever the method, the same gates apply before every production release — SIT and UAT sign-off, and the change request at CAB. In a release-driven environment, squads can run agile while the release train keeps fixed governance checkpoints.

## SM — 60s
> I care about predictability and speed, not methodology labels. Where scope and budget are fixed — regulatory programmes, budget commitments — waterfall-style control makes sense. Where the product is still being discovered, agile iterations are faster. I've run all three models: waterfall governance on our customer-360 platform, agile with change control on ISO 20022, and a hybrid at Golden Bridge. I pick by risk and by how stable the requirements are, and I keep one set of release gates across all of them.
