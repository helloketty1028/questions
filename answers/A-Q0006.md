## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 挑 2–3 个，按轮次选：HR 轮问岗位背景和流程，用人主管轮问工作内容，高管轮问战略。
2. 每个问题都要能帮你判断岗位，或者给下一轮准备素材。
3. 最后问下一步和时间线。

## 30s
> HR 轮固定三问：
> 1. "Is this a new role, or a replacement?"
> 2. "Who does this role report to, and how does it work with the squad leads and vendors?"
> 3. "What would success look like in the first six months?"
> 收尾："What are the next steps and the timeline?"（若薪资没谈清，补问："What is the job grade and the budgeted range?"）

## ATL — 60s
> - "What's the biggest technical risk in the platform today?"
> - "How are architecture decisions made, and who has the final say?"
> - "How much of the role is hands-on design and code versus coordination?"
> - "Which part of the stack would this role own first, and what's the biggest engineering challenge there right now?"
> - "Where is the team on AI-assisted engineering — what's working, and what's the blocker you'd want this person to solve?"

## TPM — 60s
> - "How many major and ad-hoc releases a month, and how many teams and vendors are involved?"
> - "Is there an on-call or after-hours rota for releases?"
> - "Where do releases most often get stuck today — testing, approvals or environments?"

## SM — 60s
> - "What are the top business priorities for technology in the next 12 months?"
> - "How are technology investment decisions made?"
> - "What does the team look like today, and where are the gaps?"
> - (CEO) "In the next 12 months, which business friction do you most want technology to solve first?"
> - (CEO) "Do you want this role more on keeping BAU stable, or on driving transformation and AI?"
> - (HRD) "How big is the tech team today, and how do you see it growing?"
> - (HRD) "Where is the team strongest today, and where does it most need help?"

## 行业角度（每个行业 1–2 句，说明同一个故事怎么换侧重点）
- **BNK**：问即将到来的监管变化对平台的影响。
- **BNK · fintech**：问牌照、增长目标和合规团队的规模。
- **INT**：问产品节奏和核心指标。
- **TEL**：问 BCC 的发布节奏，以及 Singtel28 带来的变化有多少落到计费。

## 追问与应对
- **Q**：（面试官反问"你为什么问这个"）/ **A**：直接说目的："It helps me see where I can add value first."
