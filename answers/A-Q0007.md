## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先说你负责什么：UOB 支付发起平台的发布计划和生产上线。
2. Major：ISO 20022 迁移，按 STAR 讲（S-001）。
3. 每次发布都过同一套门禁：SIT/UAT sign-off 和变更控制。
4. Ad-hoc：P1–P3 事故后的协调和永久修复（X-06）。
5. 收尾：发布节奏可以不同，门禁是同一套。

## 30s
> At UOB I own release planning and rollout for our cross-border payment platform. The biggest release was the ISO 20022 migration: a live platform, strict cut-off windows and a fixed SWIFT deadline. I chose a phased coexistence over a big-bang cutover. We went live in May and August 2025, ahead of the deadline, and retired coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption.

## ATL — 60s
> The hard part was technical: the platform was live, and old MT and new MX messages had to run side by side. I designed a phased, blue-green style coexistence instead of a big-bang cutover, with parallel traffic management and cutover-readiness gates, so we could move flows in stages and always keep a way back. I also designed the hub and wrote its core modules, including the processing of payment status results returned by downstream systems — which matters a lot when two message formats coexist. MX-rich went live in May and August 2025, ahead of SWIFT's November deadline, and we retired coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption.

## TPM — 60s
> **S** — At UOB I manage releases for our cross-border payment platform. The biggest was the ISO 20022 migration: a live platform, strict cut-off times, dependencies on channels, reference data, downstream systems and vendors, and a fixed SWIFT deadline.
> **T** — I owned delivery: on time, with no business disruption.
> **A** — I chose a phased coexistence instead of a big-bang cutover, tracked risks on RAID and reported to the steering committee. Every release passed SIT and UAT sign-off, and before each production release I prepared the change request and presented it at CAB.
> **R** — We went live in May and August 2025, ahead of the November deadline, and retired coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption. Upgrades have shipped every two to three months since.

## SM — 60s
> The key decision was about risk. A big-bang cutover would have been faster, but it would have put a live payment platform at risk inside cut-off windows, with a fixed SWIFT deadline and many vendor and third-party dependencies. I chose a phased coexistence: more effort, but a way back at every step. I governed it through RAID and steering-committee reporting, and held every release to the same gates — SIT and UAT sign-off and CAB approval. Result: MX-rich live in May and August 2025, ahead of SWIFT's deadline, coexistence retired in August 2026 with no service interruption, and a steady release cadence every two to three months since.
