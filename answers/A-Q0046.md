Rate limiter: a token bucket with a refill rate and capacity; make it thread-safe with synchronized or an AtomicLong; mention a sliding-window alternative and Redis for a distributed limiter. Aggregation: transactions.stream().collect(groupingBy(Tx::account, reducing(BigDecimal.ZERO, Tx::amount, BigDecimal::add))) — use BigDecimal, never double, for money.
