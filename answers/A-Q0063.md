## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 按岗位给三个价值点，每个配一个证据。
2. ATL：动手的架构 + 生产中的 AI。
3. TPM：受监管的交付 + 发布治理 + 供应商。
4. SM：大行的严谨 + 初创公司从 0 到 1 + AI。
5. 不说 CTO 是曾任职位；可以说 "head-of-technology roles, 2016–2020"。

## 30s
> Three things. Tier-1 bank rigour — I took a live payment platform through the ISO 20022 migration with no service interruption. Start-up speed — between 2016 and 2020 I held head-of-technology roles, building teams and platforms from scratch. And applied AI in production — I designed and built an AI code-review service inside a regulated bank. That combination fits a role that needs to move fast and stay safe.

## ATL — 60s
> Hands-on architecture and applied AI. I design systems and still write their core modules — on our payment hub I wrote transaction initiation, the permission matrix and the processing of downstream status results. I've architected platforms from zero too, like a multi-tenant SaaS on Spring Cloud and Kubernetes at Golden Bridge. And I've put AI into production safely: an AI code-review service in a regulated bank, with an estimated 80% cut in senior review time. So I can bring both the bank's discipline and modern engineering practice to your platform.

## TPM — 60s
> Delivery you can trust on business-critical platforms. I owned the ISO 20022 programme end to end — live ahead of SWIFT's deadline, coexistence retired with no service interruption, and upgrades every two to three months since. I prepare and present every production release at CAB myself, and I chair governance for four IT service vendors. And I'm technical enough to read the risk in a change, because I still review code every day. That's what I'd bring: releases that land safely and on time.

## SM — 60s
> Three things together. Tier-1 governance: I lead 25 engineers across two platform lines at UOB, with decision or joint-decision authority on technology investment, vendors and SOWs. Start-up building: between 2016 and 2020 I held head-of-technology roles, building organisations and platforms from scratch. And innovation that actually ships: I designed and built an AI code-review service in production. Most candidates bring one of those. A growing financial company needs all three — speed, control and the ability to build.
