## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. Hire：按团队缺口招人，不按头衔招。
2. Assign：定目标、截止日和红线；怎么做由他决定。
3. Appraise：平时就做 1:1、早给反馈，年底没有意外。
4. Keep：成长、ownership、有意义的工作；薪资我会去争取公平。
5. 结果段：只用真实结果，没有就删。

## 30s
> I hire for the gap in the team, not for a title. I assign clear ownership: the goal, the deadline and the line they can't cross — how they do it is up to them. I appraise continuously through regular one-to-ones, so nothing at year end is a surprise. And I keep good people with growth, ownership and meaningful work. Pay matters, and I'll argue for fair pay, but people stay when they can see where they're going.

## TPM — 60s
> （小团队版）First, I look at where the company is going and build the team around the business need — the team size isn't fixed, it grows with the business. In a small team, one person covers more areas, so I play to each person's strengths. But the rules don't get smaller: in a regulated business, approvals, access and the audit trail stay the same at any size. People stay when they believe in what the company is doing, when they grow as fast as the company, and when they can see the road ahead. I tell them what comes next, so they can.

Assign: every person owns a clear piece — I set the goal, the deadline and the line they can't cross, and how they do it is up to them. Appraise: I don't wait for year end. I run regular one-to-ones and mentoring, and give feedback early and clearly, so at year end nothing is a surprise. Keep: pay matters, and I'll argue for fair pay, but good people stay for growth, ownership and work that matters. I mentor them on the technical side, because I'm still in the code every day.
