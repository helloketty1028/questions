## ATL — 60s
> Two examples. On our payment hub, the challenge was end-to-end latency under high load. The work was in three places: tuning the thread pools, tuning the JVM, and removing synchronous blocking calls from the processing path. Latency came down, though I don't have a precise before-and-after figure I can quote. At VV Technology I designed for performance from the start: Spring Cloud microservices, Kafka for asynchronous processing, and caching with cache-breakdown protection, so requests stayed off the database — that brought service processing to the millisecond level. The approach is the same each time: measure first, find where the time is really spent, and fix that rather than guess.
