## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）

2. 速度从别处拿：砍重复劳动、把控制做进流水线、共用部分只建一次。
3. AI 工具替工程师做手工活（PA-07）。
4. 证据：ISO 20022 提前于截止日上线、无中断；每 2–3 个月一次发布。

In practice: agree the MVP scope against the real man-days, so we don't over-commit; build the controls into the pipeline, so testing and security evidence come out automatically; and have the change package ready early — I prepare it and present it at CAB myself, with the go-live plan, risk controls, rollback plan and SIT and UAT sign-off. ## SM — 60s
> Compliance is not negotiable — that's the line. So I don't buy speed by cutting compliance; I buy it by cutting repeat work. At UOB, the controls are built into the pipeline — quality gates, automated regression and penetration testing — and we run secure SDLC in line with MAS TRM, so evidence is produced as we work, not collected at the end. I prepare the change request and present every production release at CAB myself, so approvals move quickly. And I built an AI code-review service that cut senior review time by an estimated 80%. That's how our ISO 20022 migration went live ahead of SWIFT's deadline, with no service interruption, and how we keep releasing every two to three months.
