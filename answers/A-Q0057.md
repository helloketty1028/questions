## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 第一句：不只是钱。
2. 五件事：目的、ownership、成长、认可、公平的问责。
3. 目标：让人感到被信任、有挑战、被看见、能成长。

## 30s
> People are motivated by more than pay. I focus on five things: clear purpose, ownership, growth, recognition, and fair accountability. When people have ownership and a chance to grow, motivation lasts much longer.

## SM — 60s
> I believe people are motivated by more than just pay. I focus on five things: clear purpose, ownership, growth, recognition, and fair accountability. First, I make sure people understand what we're building and the business impact behind it. Then I give capable people ownership and the freedom to make decisions. I create chances for people to grow technically and to move into leadership roles. At the same time, I recognise good contributions, and I make expectations and accountability very clear. My goal is an environment where people feel trusted, challenged, recognised, and able to grow. When people have both ownership and growth, motivation becomes much more sustainable.
