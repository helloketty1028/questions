## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 按产出管，不按出勤管。
2. 一切写下来：决定、进度、阻塞都在 Jira 和 Confluence 里。
3. 跨时区：保护重叠时段，用来做需要一起讨论的事。
4. 证据：印度、泰国、印尼团队跨时区协作，5 年以上 offshore 管理（X-02）。

## 30s
> I manage on output, not attendance: clear goals, clear delivery dates, and I look at what comes out. Everything gets written down — decisions, progress and blockers live in Jira and Confluence. And across time zones, I protect the overlap hours for anything that needs people together.

## TPM — 60s
> For delivery, a remote team needs the plan to be visible to everyone without a meeting. Every item has an owner and a date in Jira, decisions are recorded in Confluence, and risks and blockers are raised in writing, not just in a call. I keep a fixed rhythm in the overlap hours for progress reviews across time zones — at UOB that's India, Thailand and Indonesia — and I judge progress by what's delivered and tested, not by status updates.

## SM — 60s
> I manage on output, not on attendance: clear goals and delivery dates, and I look at what comes out. Second, everything gets written down — decisions, progress and blockers live in Jira and Confluence. If something only happened in an office conversation, the remote half of the team never had it. Third, time zones. At UOB I work closely with teams in India, Thailand and Indonesia, and I've managed offshore teams for over five years. So I protect the overlap hours for things that need people together — design reviews, progress calls, hard conversations — and outside that window, people do the work they can finish on their own.
