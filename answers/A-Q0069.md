## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 角色：PO（负责 backlog）、scrum master / squad lead、跨职能团队（开发、测试、BA）。
2. 仪式：sprint planning、每日站会、backlog refinement、sprint review、retrospective。

4. 你的证据：GBD 从零建立 Scrum；UOB 在银行变更门禁内跑 Agile。
5. 多团队时：依赖进 RAID，在 steering 层面协调。

## 30s
> Roles: a product owner who owns the backlog, a scrum master or squad lead who runs the ceremonies, and a cross-functional team of developers, testers and a BA. Ceremonies: sprint planning, a short daily stand-up, backlog refinement, a sprint review with the business, and a retrospective. Tools: Jira and Confluence. At Golden Bridge I set Scrum up from scratch; at UOB our squads run agile inside the bank's change-control gates.

## TPM — 60s
> At UOB each of my teams runs as a squad with a squad lead; the product owner and BA bring the requirements, and developers and testers deliver. We run the standard ceremonies — planning, a daily stand-up to unblock work, refinement, a review with the business, and a retrospective to fix how we work. Jira holds the backlog and Confluence the decisions. Because a payment project spans many application teams, cross-team dependencies don't stay inside the sprint: they go into the programme RAID and get resolved at steering level. At Golden Bridge I introduced Scrum from scratch inside a hybrid model — waterfall for scope and budget, sprints for the build.
