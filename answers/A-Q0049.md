## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先给证据：我在初创公司做过（一人多岗）。
2. 杠杆一：buy vs build——不能拉开差距的就买。
3. 杠杆二：按风险和数据敏感度分工——敏感的留给自己人，其余可以外包，但要治理。
4. 杠杆三：用工具和 AI 放大人手（PA-07）。
5. 收尾：靠杠杆，不靠人头；同时不把人榨干。

## 30s
> I've done this before — at start-ups one person covers three jobs. I'd use three levers. Buy what doesn't differentiate us — cloud, identity checks, monitoring — and build only what makes us different. Sort the work by risk and data sensitivity: anything sensitive or high-risk stays with our own people, the rest can go to vendors. And multiply people with tooling — like the AI code-review service I built.

## SM — 60s
> I've done this at start-ups: at VV and Golden Bridge I built the technology organisation and the platform from scratch, where one person covers three jobs. With a lean team I'd use three levers. First, build versus buy: anything that doesn't set us apart — cloud infrastructure, identity checks, monitoring — we buy, so the team spends its time only on what makes us different. Second, sort the work by risk and data sensitivity: sensitive or high-risk work stays with our own people; the rest can go to vendors, governed properly — I chair governance for four IT service vendors today. Third, multiply people with tooling: the AI code-review service I built cut senior review time by an estimated 80%. Leverage, not headcount.
