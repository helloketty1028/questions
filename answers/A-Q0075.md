## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 先确认题意：问的是发现排不下的那一刻。
2. 早：一有数据就说，不等到已经晚了。
3. 诚实：完整说明交付了什么、风险在哪、原因是什么。
4. 带方案：A 缩范围保日期；B 全范围但延期，并量化影响；C 分阶段（MVP 按期，其余下一批）。
5. 决定由干系人做，你负责让他们带着正确的信息、有足够时间做决定。

## 30s
> Three moves: early, honest, with options. Early — as soon as the data tells me full scope won't fit, not when it's already late. Honest — I take the real picture to the PM and business owner: what's done, what's at risk, and why. With options — usually reduced scope on the date, full scope with a quantified slip, or a phased release with the MVP on the date. The stakeholder makes the call; my job is to make sure they make it with the right information and in time.

## TPM — 60s
> Just to confirm — you mean the moment we realise the scope won't fit the fixed date? Then three moves: early, honest, with options. Early: as soon as the data shows it, not when it's already late. Honest: I take the real picture to the PM and the business owner — what's delivered, what's at risk, and why — no sugar-coating, because that destroys credibility for the next conversation. With options: A, reduced scope on the original date — which features must ship and which can wait; B, full scope with a defined slip and the impact quantified; or C, a phased release — the MVP on the date, the rest in the next drop. It's the same discipline I use when my two platform lines collide: agree the MVP, confirm the real dependencies, set a sequence everyone accepts.

## SM — 60s
> I make sure bad news travels fast and comes with choices. If a fixed date is at risk, I want to know as soon as the data shows it, and I want the delivery lead to bring me options, not just the problem: cut scope, move the date with a quantified impact, or phase the release. Then the business owner decides with full information. On regulated deadlines like our ISO 20022 programme, the date doesn't move, so scope and phasing are the levers — which is exactly why we ran it as a phased coexistence.
