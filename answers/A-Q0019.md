## 答题逻辑（中文，3–5 步）
1. 第一阶段：把这个岗位做好（打消"马上想升职走人"的顾虑）。
2. 第二阶段：改进这个岗位所在的机制或平台。
3. 第三阶段：自然地扩大范围（不强求）。

## 30s
> First, to do this role very well and become the person the team trusts with its most critical work. Then to improve how that work is done. If that grows into a broader remit over time, great — but my focus is this role first.

## ATL — 60s
> In the first year, I want to get deep in the domain and the system, and become the architect the platform relies on. Over the next two to three years, I'd like to raise the engineering bar — architecture standards, quality gates, and AI-assisted engineering done safely, the way I did with the code-review service at UOB. Staying hands-on is part of the plan, not something I want to grow out of. If the role grows into leading other architects, great — but the technical depth stays.

## TPM — 60s
> In the first year, I want to be the person [company] trusts with its most critical changes — making releases predictable and auditable. In years two and three, I'd improve the release process itself, for example by automating readiness checks and quality gates so more of the evidence comes straight from the pipeline. If that grows into broader delivery leadership over time, great, but my focus is to do this role very well first.

## SM — 60s
> In three to five years I'd like to have built a technology team here that delivers reliably and moves faster than today — clear ownership, safe change, and the right mix of in-house and vendor capacity. I'd like technology to be seen as a driver of business results, not a cost centre, and to have introduced practices like AI-assisted engineering in a governed way. That's what I'd measure myself against.
