## 30s
> Building our AI code-review service. As the team grew, senior engineers were spending too long on repeat reviews and quality was inconsistent. I designed and built a service that runs a headless Claude Code agent on every pull request, alongside SonarQube, with one rule: the AI advises, humans decide. It's been in production since May 2026, and we estimate it cut senior review time by about 80%. What I'm proudest of is that it's governed, not just clever.

## ATL — 60s
> Our AI code-review service, because I built it myself. A Spring Boot service picks up each pull request, takes the three-dot diff and runs a headless Claude Code agent that reviews security, performance, correctness and maintainability, next to SonarQube; comments go back on the PR, and a human still approves every merge. I tuned it with prompt A/B testing and took it through formal architecture and risk approval. It's been live since May 2026, and we estimate senior review time is down about 80%. I'm proud that it's real engineering inside a regulated bank, not a demo.

## TPM — 60s
> Delivering our ISO 20022 migration. The platform was live, payments had strict cut-offs, and SWIFT's deadline was fixed. I chose a phased coexistence over a big-bang cutover, ran the programme on RAID with steering-committee reporting, and held every release to SIT and UAT sign-off. We went live in May and August 2025, ahead of the deadline, and retired coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption. I'm proud that nobody outside the team noticed — which is exactly what success looks like in payments.

## SM — 60s
> Turning AI into a governed engineering capability. Code review had become a bottleneck as the team grew. I designed and built an AI review service, set the rule that it advises and never merges, aligned it with MAS FEAT principles, and took it through formal architecture and risk approval before production in May 2026. We estimate it cut senior review time by about 80%. What I'm proudest of isn't the AI — it's showing a regulated bank how to adopt it safely.
